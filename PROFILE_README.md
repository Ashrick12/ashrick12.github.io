### 🌟 Featured Engineering Projects

#### 🚀 [Scratch-Built Sounding Rocket](https://ashrick12.github.io/#flagship) *(First Test Flight Complete &bull; Rocket Recovered Intact)*
* Flew maiden test flight on an Estes D12-3 motor at Freestone Park: actual total flight time of 27.06 s matched OpenRocket 5° tilt simulation within 0.26 s, with ~4.79 s time to apogee and parachute deployment at ~5.0 s.
* Rocket recovered intact with no visible structural damage; positive washer motor retention and Nomex piston performed as intended.
* Custom 36.4" (BT-80) airframe built in gold livery with 3D-printed nose cone, 1.60 cal stability margin, and 309g measured empty airframe mass.
* Measured physical airframe tube variance (65.5mm ID) with digital calipers to redesign 3D-printed coupler (38g) and motor mount (46g) for a precision friction fit.
* Tested RP2040 flight computer logic on breadboard in MicroPython; currently wiring BMP390 barometer and MPU-6500 IMU for Flight 2 onboard logging.
* *[View Interactive Case Study](https://ashrick12.github.io/#flagship) &bull; [Watch Flight Video](https://ashrick12.github.io/#flagship)*
