Predicted Trajectory Replay & Simulation Readout

OpenRocket generates detailed numerical simulation output and standard 2D plots. This client-side tool transforms those CSV exports into an interactive 3D flight replay over calibrated satellite terrain to evaluate predicted trajectory turnover and landing dispersion.

  • Simulation Data Parser & Interpolation: Ingests raw OpenRocket CSV files with automatic delimiter and event detection. Uses binary-search sample lookup and linear interpolation (lerp) to generate smooth 60 FPS replay from discrete 10–50 ms simulation samples at 0.25×–10× playback.
  • Calibrated Launch Environment: Maps a 4095×4095 px aerial orthophoto of Freestone Park (0.1247 m/px, 510.7 m domain) to OpenRocket's coordinate conventions (+X East, −Z North), providing a calibrated visualization environment with 50 m range rings.
  • Procedural Vehicle Attitude Visualization: Procedurally aligns the rendered airframe along the instantaneous trajectory velocity tangent (ΔP/Δt) through boost and apogee turnover, deploying a 12-line parachute with modeled descent pendulum oscillation.
  • Simulation Data HUD & Procedural Audio: Displays predicted altitude, velocity, acceleration, thrust, drag, and downrange drift. Uses Web Audio API synthesis to modulate motor rumble from thrust (N) and airflow rush from total velocity (m/s).
  • Pre-Loaded Datasets: Pre-configured with simulation models for Ashton's sounding rocket builds, including the Freestone Park Estes D12-3 maiden flight baseline and the Aerotech F32T-6 mid-power upgrade trajectory.
